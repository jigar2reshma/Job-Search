## 🗺️ Career Launch Workflow

<details>
<summary><b>Phase 1: Foundation & Self-Assessment (Click to expand)</b></summary>

* **Define Your "Why":** Identify core values and set 1, 3, and 5-year goals.
* **Skills Audit:** Inventory technical skills, soft skills, and academic projects.
* **Target Roles:** Research job titles and analyze descriptions for "skill gaps."
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Phase 2: Professional Toolkit</b></summary>

* **📄 Master Resume:** Create an ATS-friendly master document.
* **🌐 LinkedIn Optimization:** Professional headshot and keyword-rich "About" section.
* **📝 Cover Letter:** Build a flexible template focused on "Why me?"
* **📂 Portfolio:** Showcase 3-5 best projects with live demos.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Phase 3: Strategy & Active Search</b></summary>

* **Job Search Strategy:** Set up targeted alerts and a tracking system (Notion/Excel).
* **Digital Networking:** Personalize LinkedIn requests and engage with industry content.
* **Informational Interviews:** Reach out to professionals in roles you admire.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Phase 4: Execution & Interviews</b></summary>

* **Application Process:** Tailor your resume for *every* submission.
* **Behavioral Prep:** Master the **STAR Method** (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
* **Technical Prep:** Review Data Structures and practice on LeetCode.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Phase 5: Closing the Loop</b></summary>

* **Post-Interview:** Send personalized thank-you emails within 24 hours.
* **Negotiation:** Review benefits and salary before signing.
* **Growth:** Set learning goals for your first 90 days.
</details>
