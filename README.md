## 🗺️ The Career Launch Workflow
*Click a phase to expand the roadmap.*

<details>
<summary><b>Phase 1: Foundation & Self-Assessment</b></summary>

* **Define Your "Why":** Identify core values and set 1, 3, and 5-year career goals.
* **Skills & Experience Audit:** Inventory Technical (Languages/Tools) and Soft Skills.
* **Target Roles:** Research job titles and analyze job descriptions for "Skill Gaps."
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Phase 2: Build Your Professional Toolkit</b></summary>

* **📄 Master Resume:** Build an ATS-friendly base resume (no complex graphics).
* **🌐 LinkedIn Optimization:** Professional headshot, keyword-rich "About" section, and custom URL.
* **📝 Cover Letter:** Create a template focusing on "Why this company?" and "Why me?"
* **📂 Portfolio:** Showcase 3-5 best projects with clear "Problem/Solution" descriptions.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Phase 3: Strategy & Active Search (The Hidden Market)</b></summary>

* **Job Search Strategy:** Set targeted alerts on LinkedIn, Indeed, and Handshake.
* **Niche Communities:** Join Slack/Discord groups (e.g., *Design Buddies*, *Reactiflux*).
* **Event Strategy:** Use **Eventbrite**, **Meetup**, and **Luma** to find Industry Mixers.
* **Informational Interviews:** Outreach to alumni via the LinkedIn "Alumni" tab.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Phase 4: Execution - Applications & Interviews</b></summary>

* **Application Process:** Tailor your Resume and Cover Letter for *every* submission.
* **Behavioral Prep:** Prepare stories using the **STAR Method** (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
* **Technical Prep:** Review core concepts and practice challenges (LeetCode/HackerRank).
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Phase 5: Closing & Continuous Growth</b></summary>

* **Post-Interview:** Send personalized thank-you emails within 24 hours.
* **Negotiation:** Review salary, benefits, and equity before accepting.
* **First 90 Days:** Set learning goals and find a mentor at your new company.
</details>
